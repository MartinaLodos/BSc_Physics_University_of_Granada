Basic Experimental Techniques is a 1st-year course of 6 ECTS in the BSc in Physics at the University of Granada, designed to introduce students to laboratory work and rigorous data analysis.

It is structured around three main pillars:
* Data Processing & Uncertainties: Descriptive statistics, probability distributions, error calculation and propagation for direct and indirect measurements, and dimensional analysis.
* Laboratory Work: 12 hands-on experiments covering mechanics, thermodynamics, electromagnetism, and optics.
- Scientific Tools: Data analysis using software (Python / spreadsheets) and scientific report writing in LaTex.
