*Mechanics and Waves* is an advanced 12 ECTs course covering Newtonian and Analytical Mechanics, Vibrations, Wave Dynamics and Special Relativity. The experiments cover the following topics.
* **Newtonian Mechanics & Non-Inertial Frames:** Rotating reference systems, fictitious forces, and rigid body dynamics (Euler equations, inertia tensor).
* **Analytical Mechanics:** Lagrangian formulation, generalized coordinates, Hamilton's equations, and central force motion.
* **Oscillations & Waves:** Damped, driven, and coupled oscillations; mechanical wave propagation, standing waves, interference, and the Doppler effect.
