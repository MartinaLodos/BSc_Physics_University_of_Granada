*Thermodynamics* is an anual course of 12 ECTs on macroscopic thermodynamics, thermal properties of matter, phase equilibria, kinetic theory, and experimental thermal measurements. Lab sessions include the following topics:
* **Fundamental Laws:** Zero, First, Second, and Third Laws of Thermodynamics; entropy, heat engines, and Carnot cycles.
* **Thermodynamic Potentials & Stability:** Internal energy, enthalpy, Maxwell relations, and stability criteria.
* **Phase Equilibria & Open Systems:** Chemical potential, first/second-order phase transitions (Clapeyron equation), and multicomponent systems.
* **Real Gases:**  Van der Waals real gases.
